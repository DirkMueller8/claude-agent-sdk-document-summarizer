## Why Remote Teams Need Written Communication

Remote teams work across time zones, so meetings are often impossible to schedule without someone joining very early or very late. Written communication solves this by letting people respond when they are at their best, not when the calendar demands it.

## Core Practices

- Write decisions down in a shared place so that anyone can find them later.
- Prefer short documents with a clear ask over long chat threads.
- State the deadline and the owner for every action item.
- Use meetings only for discussions that truly need real-time back and forth.

## Common Pitfalls

Teams often over-rely on chat tools, which scatter decisions across many channels. Messages without context force readers to guess, causing delays and rework. Without a single source of truth, new team members struggle to understand why past decisions were made.

## Measuring Success

A healthy written culture shows up in fewer status meetings, faster onboarding of new hires, and fewer repeated questions. Teams can track these through onboarding time and the number of recurring questions in support channels.
